Design rationale

01 · Home

PurposeFirst impression sells the core promise — "see it on you, before you buy it" — with a single dominant call to action.
Key features
  • Hero CTA straight into the try-on journey
  • Category chips & trending looks for browsing
  • AI Stylist entry point (differentiator)
Marketing angleReduces purchase anxiety — the #1 driver of fashion e-commerce returns (~30% industry average). The hero speaks benefit, not feature.

02 · Clothing & accessories

PurposeLightweight catalogue where users pick a garment and layer accessories before trying on. Tap a card to select it.
Key features
  • Filterable product grid with prices
  • Accessory add-ons (necklace, bag, sunglasses) that carry into the try-on
  • Add to cart & Buy now with live bag counter
  • Selected state clearly marked
Marketing angleAccessory layering raises average order value through cross-selling at the moment of highest engagement.

03 · Size & style profile

PurposeCollect five measurements once, then recommend S / M / L with a confidence score. Try changing the numbers — the recommendation is computed live.
Key features
  • Height, weight, bust, waist, hips
  • Fit preference (slim / regular / oversized) shifts the recommendation
  • Fashion-style tags feed the AI stylist
  • Clear privacy note builds trust
Marketing angleFirst-party data capture: measurements + style preference are high-value segmentation data for personalised campaigns.

04 · Upload photo

PurposeTwo clear paths — camera or gallery — with guidance that maximises try-on quality. Tap either option to simulate a successful upload.
Key features
  • Camera with pose guide / gallery picker
  • Photo quality tips
  • Body detection confirmation with mapped key points
  • 24-hour auto-deletion promise
Marketing angleThe privacy promise is a trust signal — surveys show data concern is the top barrier to virtual try-on adoption.

05 · Virtual try-on

PurposeThe hero moment: garment draped on the user's photo. Swap colours, sizes and accessories in real time — try them here.
Key features
  • Live garment overlay on the detected body
  • Colour swatches & S/M/L toggle (watch the drape change)
  • Accessories toggle on/off
Marketing angleInteractive product experience lengthens session time and is inherently shareable — the engine of organic social reach.

06 · Fit results

PurposeAn honest verdict: fit score, per-zone breakdown, and a final size recommendation. Try size L or S on screen 05, then re-analyze — the score reacts.
Key features
  • Fit score gauge (poor → highly fit)
  • Zone chips: shoulders, bust, waist, hips
  • Final size recommendation with reasoning
  • Add to cart & Buy now (price shown) right at the moment of conviction
  • Save & share actions
Marketing angleHonest fit scoring cuts returns and builds brand trust; the shareable result card turns every customer into media.

07 · Your bag

PurposeA persistent bag that collects looks from anywhere in the journey — shop, try-on, or fit results. Open it any time from the bag icon or bottom nav.
Key features
  • Item cards with photo, chosen size & colour
  • Remove items, live subtotal + delivery fee
  • Empty state nudges back to shopping
Marketing angleThe bag is a remarketing surface — abandoned-bag reminders are among the highest-ROI emails in fashion e-commerce (~40% open rates).

08 · Checkout & payment

PurposeA single-screen, low-friction checkout: delivery details, payment choice, order summary, one tap to confirm.
Key features
  • Card, mobile wallet, and cash-on-delivery options
  • Order summary with per-item pricing and delivery fee
  • Instant confirmation screen with order number & tracking
Marketing angle~70% of fashion carts are abandoned at checkout; offering cash-on-delivery and local wallets (key for the Sri Lankan market) directly attacks the top abandonment reasons.

✦ AI Stylist (extra)

PurposeDifferentiator beyond the brief: conversational styling matched to body shape, occasion and declared style tags.
Key features
  • Occasion-based outfit suggestions with style-match %
  • One tap from suggestion to try-on
  • Wardrobe, fit history & social share cards as further extras
Marketing anglePersonalisation at scale — the stylist creates repeat visits (retention) rather than one-off try-ons, and its suggestions are a native ad surface for partner brands.
